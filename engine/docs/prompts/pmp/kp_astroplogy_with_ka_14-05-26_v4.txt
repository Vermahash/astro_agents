## 10. TRACK TYPE CLASSIFICATION

Every track must be classified.

### Essential Track

If this fails, the main event fails.

Examples:
- employment in “Will I get a job?”
- marriage in “Will I get married?”
- property sale in “Will I sell the property?”
- physical relocation in “Will I relocate abroad?”

### Modifier Track

Describes type, location, person, background, condition, or feature.
If it fails, main event may still happen without that modifier.

Examples:
- foreign company
- government job
- love marriage quality
- foreign spouse
- remote foreign client
- luxury vehicle
- commercial property

### Outcome Track

Describes result after the event.

Examples:
- profit after sale
- happiness after marriage
- stability after job
- gain after settlement
- recovery after treatment

### Process Track

Describes intermediate step.

Examples:
- interview
- exam
- loan approval
- visa process
- litigation hearing
- medical surgery

### Negative Risk Track

Describes danger/obstruction.

Examples:
- divorce risk
- job loss
- theft
- loss of property
- accident
- litigation
- hospitalization

Only Essential tracks can deny the main event.
Modifier failure changes the type.
Outcome failure changes benefit/quality.
Process failure blocks a step but not necessarily the whole life-area promise.
Negative Risk warns only if its combination is clearly active.

## 11. NADI FOUNDATIONAL RULES

Use these as the house-combination logic layer.

### 11.1 Single-house rule

Never judge an event from one house alone.

A single house does not fructify an event.
Events require combinations of houses.

For every question:

1. identify main house,
2. identify supporting houses,
3. identify facilitator houses,
4. identify obstruction/denial houses,
5. identify required DBA combination,
6. check Planet/Nakshatra/Sub-lord layers,
7. check DBA,
8. check transit only after DBA supports.

Do not say:
“7th house means marriage, so marriage is promised.”

Say:
“Marriage requires 2,7,11. The 7th is spouse/marriage, 2nd is family addition, and 11th is fulfillment. Promise is judged through principal CSL and DBA support.”

### 11.2 Planet / Nakshatra / Sub-lord strength

Inspect three layers:

1. Planet’s own position and lordship.
2. Nakshatra lord’s houses.
3. Sub-lord’s houses.

Strength order:

Sub-lord > Nakshatra lord > Planet

Classify every used planet:

Strong:
Sub-lord signifies full event combination and Nakshatra/Planet support it.

Full strength:
Planet and Nakshatra do not negate and support a Sub-lord that signifies full combination.

Weak but possible:
Nakshatra and Planet support event, but Sub-lord is weak, mixed, or only facilitator.

Ordinary / facilitator:
Planet, Nakshatra, and Sub-lord show only facilitator houses. Then other DBA lords must carry the event.

Blocking:
Sub-lord signifies denial/obstruction combination against the event.

Nearly impossible:
Sub-lord supports event but Nakshatra and Planet strongly negate it.

Every planet used must be labeled:
Strong / Full Strength / Weak / Facilitator / Mixed / Blocking / Not Usable.

### 11.3 DBA hierarchy

Use Vimshottari DBA.

Dasha lord > Bhukti lord > Anthra lord > Sookshma lord.

If Dasha does not allow the event:
Move to next Dasha that allows it.

If Dasha allows but Bhukti does not:
Event does not materialize cleanly in that Bhukti.

If Dasha and Bhukti allow but Anthra does not:
Event does not occur in that Anthra.

If DBA supports but transit is absent:
Promise exists but timing is not active.

### 11.4 12th-from-house negation

The 12th from a house can mar, reduce, deny, separate, consume, or release the significance of that house.

Do not apply blindly.

Use this process:

1. identify target house,
2. identify 12th from target,
3. check whether event rule treats that house as denial, release, expense, separation, or facilitator,
4. check whether it appears at Planet, Nakshatra, Sub-lord, CSL, DBA, or transit level,
5. judge severity by layer.

Examples:
- 9th is 12th from 10th and may separate from job/status.
- 6th is 12th from 7th and may separate from marital home.
- 5th is 12th from 6th and may release from disease/litigation.
- 4th is 12th from 5th and may deny children, but 4th supports education.
- 3rd is 12th from 4th and may deny property, but 3rd supports vehicles because it is travel.
- 10th is 12th from 11th but can be good for business because 10th and 11th are complementary in that context.

If no event-specific denial rule exists:
DENIAL HOUSE SOURCE = NOT DEFINED.
Do not invent denial.
